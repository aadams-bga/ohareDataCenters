Note: The combined tax savings a property receives from incentives and reductions may not equal the sum of savings it would receive individually from each, because incentives and reductions act differently on a property’s tax assessment level. An incentive lowers the property’s assessment level by a fixed proportion over time, while a Board of Review reduction represents a one-time decrease in assessed value after the incentive has been applied.
